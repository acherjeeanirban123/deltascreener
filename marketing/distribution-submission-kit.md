## Standard copy (use everywhere)

**Name:** DeltaScreener
**Tagline (60 chars):** Free US stock screener with a plain-text query language
**Short description:** Screen 4,900+ NYSE & NASDAQ stocks by typing queries like "ROE > 20 AND Debt to Equity < 0.5". 100 prebuilt screens, live fundamentals, Excel export. Free, no sign-up.
**Long description:** DeltaScreener is a free stock screener for the US market with a twist: instead of clicking through dropdown filters, you type your screen as a plain-text query — `Market Cap > 10000 AND P/E < 15 AND ROE > 18` — the way screener.in popularized for Indian stocks. It covers ~4,900 NYSE and NASDAQ stocks with 50+ fundamental metrics, ships with 100 ready-made screens (value, growth, dividend, quality, momentum), and every screen has a shareable public page with live results. Saved screens, watchlists, and email alerts are free; Pro ($5/mo or $39/yr) adds unlimited screens/alerts, full Excel exports, and 10-year financials.
